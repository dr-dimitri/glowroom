The LC-03 developer-only CPU reference adapts the following source algorithms
from the pinned Lightcraft source. PicEdit selects the upstream MIT license
option for these portions. The complete upstream MIT license and NOTICE are
preserved verbatim in licenses/lightcraft/LICENSE-MIT and
licenses/lightcraft/NOTICE. The upstream NOTICE describes the entire upstream
product; the separate upstream materials it lists are not imported by this
evaluation.

crates/pipeline/src/local.rs:
  log_lum; guided, guided_coeffs, guided_apply, guided_fast,
  guided_fast_step; median_hv, median_row, transpose; base_detail_radius,
  tone_base_detail, tone_base; BASE_DETAIL, BASE_DETAIL_MAX, BASE_EPS;
  base sigma selection from plane_sigmas.
crates/pipeline/src/finish.rs:
  The local highlights/shadows weights and RGB exp2(delta) application in
  finish_with. No complete finish pipeline or global ToneMap is imported.
crates/raster/src/blur.rs:
  box_radii, radii_for, box_row, box_v and gaussian (three-box approximation
  with clamped edges).
crates/raster/src/resample.rs:
  weights, resize, Filter and Pixel behavior for Box downsampling and bilinear
  coefficient upsampling used by guided_fast. The optional oracle extracts the
  original module without tests (also compiling support/eval, madd, fit and half,
  but calling only Box/Bilinear resize); the Swift adapter implements these two
  filters.
crates/pipeline/src/tests_tone.rs and tests_local.rs:
  Median, fine-line, guided-filter, constant-field and edge fixture concepts.

Modifications in PicEdit (2026): Swift/array adaptation; bounded execution and
cancellation through the existing developer harness; explicit developed-pixel
extended-linear-sRGB contract with an explicit RGB bridge into Rec.2020 before
its original luminance weights are used; scalar gain applied to original sRGB
(commuting with the linear bridge); explicit native/image-space scale;
coverage-weighted median, means and resampling for independent alpha handling;
and isolated tone-stage evaluation without Lightcraft's RAW development, global
tone model, contrast/whites/blacks, color, sharpening or denoising pipeline.
Details, numerical validation and limitations are documented in
../../docs/LIGHTCRAFT-TONE-EVALUATION.md. Upstream extracted oracle functions
remain temporary build inputs; their input-file hashes and frozen results are
recorded beside the oracle.

No upstream photographs, fonts, branding, camera matrices, neural model code,
weights or other assets are transferred. No Rust dependency is added to the
application or its normal build/test path.
